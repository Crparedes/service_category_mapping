# Status:
  - Several quantity mappings were confirmed.
  - Many changes are pending on the BIPM side, relating to defining new quantities.
  - Quantities for temperature difference should be defined for all temperature scale.

# Challenges:
  - **Celsius temperature** and **ITS-90 temperature (°C)** should not be quantities independent from **Thermodynamic temperature** and **ITS-90 temperature (K)**. Those are the same quantity expressed in different scales.
    - Both redundant quantities are mentioned in the SI Brochure, the ISO 80000 series, and the *mise en pratique* of the Kelvin.
  - Units for relative humidity:
    - Request: use **%rh** as an unit. Discussion is ongoing.
    - CP-BIPM: Recommendation to keep the unit one, where percentage will be accepted as a coherent symbol in the future (not yet implemented in the )
  - Some SC are for obsolete technologies: Psychrometers (T/H-3.2.1). This should be defined by the CC in the SCs document.
