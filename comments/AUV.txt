# Status:
  - Most quantity mappings were confirmed. The remaining portion belong to SCs that are 'obsolete' or 'not yet in use' and must be deleted by the BIPM.
  - Most SCs have a single quantity, but some others accept either relative values (in dB, specifying reference conditions) or absolute values (e.g. in V/Pa). The system can accept more than one quantity per SC.

# Challenges:
  - Gustavo Ripper and Thomas Bruns:

    The services related to CCAUV include the following areas

      - **V**- **V**ibration
      - **A**- **A**coustics (Sound in air)
      - **U**- **U**ltrasound
      - **W**- Under**w**ater acoustics

    A, U, V and W are used to identify the KCs carried out in these four areas.
    Some time ago, for some unknown reason the U services in the KCDB started to be identified as W, where W included both Ultrasound and Underwater acoustics.
    This is a little strange, because the CCAUV services are now referring to W services instead of U.
    If we need to keep only 3 identifiers, then I find U better because it refers both to the first letter of Ultrasound and of Underwater, but if it is possible to the KCDB include 4 identifiers, then would it be possible to reinstate the A, U, V and W?
      - "A"= (infra) sound in **a**ir,
      - "U"= **u**ltrasound (air and water)
      - "W"= "hydroacoustics under **w**ater"
      - "V"= "mechanical **v**ibration and shock"
