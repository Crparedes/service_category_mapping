# Status:
  - Several quantity mappings were confirmed. Work is pending with the BIPM to add additional requested quantities.
  - Most service categories allow absolute quantities (e.g. voltage in V) and relative quantities (e.g. using V/V).  The system can accept more than one quantity per SC.
  - Many service categories have more than one type of quantity. This is also accepted by the system.
